# Bonaca et al. 2012
Two epochs of HST imaging separated by several years to measure proper motions of Sag. stream stars

Concluded that stream stars have coherent patterns in P.M. that match orbital models

# Sohn et al. 2015
Used stellar population models and matched filters to extract stream stars with SDSS imaging

Concluded that Sag. stream can be detected photometrically -- shows up as an overdensity along the isochrone
